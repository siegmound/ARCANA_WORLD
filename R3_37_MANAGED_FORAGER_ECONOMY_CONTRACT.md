# ARCANA WorldSim v0.6D1-R3.37
## Intensive Managed-Forager Demography, Settlement Persistence & Exchange Economy

### Parent authority
R3.37 is downstream of SEALED R3.36 and may execute only when R3.36 resolves to `INTENSIVE_MANAGED_FORAGER_PATHWAY`. It also consumes SEALED R3.30 census/group ABM, R3.31 technological ecology, and R3.32 regional/subsistence state. Parent artifacts are immutable.

### Scientific purpose
Resolve whether a non-agricultural managed-forager pathway can support persistent central places, storage/buffering, cooperative specialization, regional exchange, redistribution and demographic resilience without materializing agriculture, cities, states, named cultures or social classes by fiat.

### Window
20 ka -> 0 ka on the exact 145-state R3.32/R3.36 axis. The 32-member ensemble and both candidate lineages are retained exactly.

### Causal state
R3.37 derives and dynamically replays:
- managed-resource support from R3.36 producer-food contributions;
- resource predictability from managed-patch/resource persistence;
- storage/buffering from R3.31 storage technology + R3.32 delayed-return economy;
- settlement persistence from shelter/site engineering, storage, continuity and predictable managed resources;
- exchange connectivity from R3.30 networks, R3.31 transport/logistics and R3.32 regional differentiation/exchange;
- cooperative specialization from R3.31/R3.32 specialization capacities and buffering;
- redistribution potential from storage + exchange + specialization;
- resource-control asymmetry pressure as a diagnostic only, never materialized inequality;
- a bounded economy-coupled census-equivalent posterior anchored to R3.30, never an archaeological headcount claim.

### Settlement/economy semantics
`persistent_central_place_potential` is not a village or town. `resource_surplus_buffer` is not a measured caloric surplus. `resource_control_asymmetry_pressure` is not a social class or hierarchy. These outputs are causal readiness/pressure states only.

### Pathway resolution
Valid outcomes include:
- `DISTRIBUTED_MANAGED_FORAGER_ECONOMY`
- `SEASONALLY_AGGREGATED_EXCHANGE_ECONOMY`
- `PERSISTENT_CENTRAL_PLACE_FORAGER_ECONOMY`
- `REGIONAL_COMPLEX_FORAGER_ECONOMY`

No outcome is targeted in advance.

### Prohibited materialization
R3.37 does not materialize agriculture, domesticates, villages, cities, states, classes, currencies, markets, named cultures, languages, religions, unique-human identity, or Deep/noetic biological coupling.

### Governance
- R3.36 pathway and no-agriculture result immutable.
- No lineage-specific rescaling.
- No agriculture threshold relaxation.
- Sensitivity is diagnostic, not a lineage-selection gate.
- Group-level weighted ABM semantics remain inherited from R3.30.
