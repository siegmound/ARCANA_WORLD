# v0.6D1-R3 — Barrier-History Binding Contract

## Purpose
Bind the rebased R2.1 natural-control macroevolution runtime to a resolved deep-time paleogeographic barrier history without inventing a new geology model.

## Authorities
1. **A1 environmental keyframes** are the endpoint authority at 210, 180, 150, 140, 130, 120, 90, 66, 60, 30 and 0 Ma.
2. **D3.2C paleogeographic-history source** is the event-reconstruction authority and is retained byte-for-byte (`SHA-256 5831f41ba9cd7bc05be0cd25f8cd84e895c6c9fa453bde7634d7b27c82394730`).
3. **R2.1** remains the authority for governed speciation/extinction/cadence semantics.
4. Deep biological coupling remains OFF in H0.

## Multi-bracket reconstruction
For each adjacent A1 pair, R3 delegates endpoint-constrained land-support reconstruction to D3.2C. Continuous climate/resource/reference fields are interpolated between endpoint fields; land support and plate transitions use the D3.2C event schedule. Derived event clusters are not independent geological observations.

Default inherited parameters:
- event phase envelope: 5–95%;
- transition width: 1 Myr;
- land-support connectivity threshold: 0.25;
- effective connectivity core threshold: 0.20;
- persistent vicariance requirement: 2 Myr.

## Initial-fragmentation rule
A component already fragmented in the 210 Ma rebaseline is **grandfathered**. It does not start a vicariance timer at t=0. It must first be observed connected; only a subsequent connected→fragmented transition can start a persistence clock. This prevents inherited ecological patchiness from being reinterpreted as a new geological event.

## Fission semantics
Persistent vicariance may materialize a daughter **deme/component only**. It preserves current species identity at fission and cannot create a new species. Speciation remains governed exclusively by the R2.1/D3.0C+D3.2B gates.

## Sensitivity requirement
Identity of mature/candidate parent lineages must be robust to reasonable barrier-history perturbations before production use. Sub-Myr timing is diagnostic and not authorially sealed.

## Production boundary
R3 authorizes the barrier-history provider and fission semantics. It does not by itself seal the complete 210→0 Ma H0 history. Long replay segments must use checkpoint/resume and be audited after local execution.
