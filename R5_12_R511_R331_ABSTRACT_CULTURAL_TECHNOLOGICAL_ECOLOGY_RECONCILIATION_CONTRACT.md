1. portable toolkit systems;
2. composite tool systems;
3. food processing and extraction;
4. thermal/environmental control;
5. shelter and site engineering;
6. transport and logistical systems;
7. storage and resource buffering;
8. cooperative specialization systems.

They are not specific artifacts, named inventions, archaeological industries, or evidence that a bow, boat, pot, loom, domesticate, settlement type or other concrete technology existed.

The R3.31 innovation/loss/cross-lineage-transfer rates, operational threshold and sensitivity multipliers remain frozen **legacy diagnostic model hyperparameters**, not measured historical rates and not canonical numeric truth.

### Cultural semantics

Inter-lineage diffusion is an opportunity/model-transfer mechanism only; it is not a claim of realized historical gene flow, ancestry, cultural borrowing event, or dated invention transfer.
