# v0.6D1-R3.36 — Producer Domestication Selection-Ecology Reassessment & Forager-vs-Food-Production Pathway Resolution

## Purpose
R3.36 resolves the remaining ecological closure problem identified by R3.35 without relaxing any R3.34/R3.35 domestication gate. The stage replays the same 20 ka→0 ka producer coevolution with explicit spatial resource concentration, temporal patch persistence, managed-patch memory, managed-population isolation, dynamic effective wild↔managed gene flow, and demographic return feedback.

## Parent authority
- R3.35 must be SEALED 29/29.
- All 36 Producer Operational Taxa and all six R3.35 genetic response axes are immutable.
- R3.34 incipient and functional-domestication thresholds are inherited exactly.
- The R3.34 90×180 Producer Resource Landscape is the only source of spatial resource concentration/persistence; R3.36 invents no new Holocene climate or vegetation field.
- Deep remains OFF and no unique human identity is materialized.

## Selection-ecology closure
For every member × sapient lineage × producer taxon, R3.36 adds:
1. local resource concentration derived from the top-decile resource-mass share of the SEALED producer landscape;
2. resource-patch persistence from weighted overlap between successive R3.34 landscape anchors;
3. managed-patch persistence as a memory state driven by contact, management, propagation and persistent resources;
4. managed-population isolation, conditioned by propagation, patch persistence and taxon selfing/clonal/propagation traits;
5. effective wild gene flow, which can be reduced by isolation but never increased above the R3.34 wild-gene-flow pressure and never forced to zero by fiat;
6. demographic return feedback from persistent managed resources and genetic improvement.

The R3.35 multivariate breeder-equation replay is rerun using this dynamic effective gene flow. Thus ecological isolation can causally change genetic response; it is not merely added to the final domestication score.

## Pathway resolution
R3.36 accepts four canonical outcomes:
- `FOOD_PRODUCTION_DOMESTICATION_PATHWAY` if robust functional domesticates and sufficient food support emerge;
- `PROTRACTED_INCIPIENT_DOMESTICATION_PATHWAY` if incipient but not robust functional domestication emerges;
- `INTENSIVE_MANAGED_FORAGER_PATHWAY` if managed/propagated resources are important but domestication gates remain unmet;
- `MIXED_FORAGING_MANAGEMENT_PATHWAY` otherwise.

No pathway is preferred in advance. Sensitivity is diagnostic only and cannot promote a taxon or pathway into the baseline.
