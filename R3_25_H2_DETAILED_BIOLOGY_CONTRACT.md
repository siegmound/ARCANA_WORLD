# v0.6D1-R3.25 — H2 Detailed Candidate Biology, Embodiment-Feasibility Envelope & H3 Priority Cohort

## Authority boundary

R3.25 consumes the **SEALED R3.24 H1 shortlist** and the SEALED R3.23 functional ensemble read-only. It also reads the R3.21 present-lineage/component registry and reduced ecological-genetic state as background diagnostics. It MUST NOT mutate H0, CHA-2, R3.23 functional values, or R3.24 H1 criteria.

H2 asks a stricter question than H1:

> Which H1 lineages retain a coherent, multi-system biological feasibility envelope when manipulation, locomotor-effector coupling, neurocognitive integration, development, social transmission, life history, ecology and action-chain requirements are considered together under uncertainty?

This is **not** a Homo-similarity test and does not commit to a human body plan.

## Emergent shortlist context

The SEALED H1 shortlist contains 12 lineages and, in the current H0 realization, all 12 are guild-4 `small_reptiloid_generalist`. R3.25 therefore explicitly includes reptile cognition/social-learning/life-history evidence in its comparative rationale and MUST NOT reject a lineage merely for being non-mammalian.

## H2 coupled biology modules

Eight coupled modules are evaluated from the 31 R3.23 primitive traits using within-ensemble cross-species percentile ranks:

1. embodied manipulation;
2. locomotor-effector compatibility;
3. neurocognitive integration;
4. learning-development integration;
5. social transmission/communication;
6. ecological-resource buffering;
7. cumulative action-chain integration;
8. life-history sustainability for prolonged learning.

No module is an observed anatomical reconstruction. They are feasibility envelopes over latent functional states.

## Missing anatomy rule

R3.25 MUST NOT invent a unique skeleton, appendage topology, brain volume, metabolic rate, reproductive mode, or sensory organ for any candidate. Instead it evaluates six generic embodiment hypotheses and reports breadth/fragility across them. Structural identity remains unresolved until a later dedicated embodiment stage if required.

## Existing reduced genetic state

R3.21 reduced genetic state is preserved semantically:

- `reduced_va_within` remains ecological-trait VA, not functional VA;
- `reduced_ancestry_covariance` remains ecological ancestry covariance;
- `reduced_neutral_segregation_potential` is used only as a background within-lineage divergence diagnostic;
- `reduced_adaptive_coordinate` remains the existing ecological adaptive coordinate.

None are repurposed into functional G matrices.

## H3 priority cohort

H2 produces a precommitted **6-lineage H3 priority cohort** from the 12 H1 candidates by ordinal consensus across non-equivalent H2 diagnostics. The cohort size is a follow-up capacity choice, not a biological pass/fail threshold. The six excluded lineages remain valid H1 candidates.

## Sensitivity and seal

The single R3.25 command performs source checks, regression, H2 detailed-biology analysis, sensitivity, integrated audit and one final seal. No R3.25A/B/C micro-stages are created.
