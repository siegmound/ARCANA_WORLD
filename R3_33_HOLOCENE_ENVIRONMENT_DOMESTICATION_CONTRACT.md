# v0.6D1-R3.33 — Holocene Environmental Resource Landscape, Ecological Partner Dynamics, Domestication Trajectories & Food-Production Emergence

## Parent authority
`PASS_R332_SUBSISTENCE_INTENSIFICATION_REGIONAL_CULTURAL_LINEAGES_MANAGED_RESOURCE_TRANSITIONS_AND_HOLOCENE_READINESS_SEALED`

## Purpose
Reconnect the two technocultural lineages to the complete H0 animal ecology and the SEALED recent paleoclimate provider. Materialize a 20→0 ka environmental-resource landscape, screen ecological-partner candidates without inventing species, and replay management → habituation → reproductive control → selective divergence trajectories.

## Environmental authority
R3.33 consumes the SEALED v0.6.1 recent-paleoclimate products directly:
- 120 ka→0 global history at 100-year cadence;
- 21, 14, 12.9, 12 and 0 ka spatial temperature, precipitation, NPP and land-mask snapshots.
Spatial fields are block-downsampled 720×1440 → WorldSim 90×180, then interpolated only between existing provider snapshots at the nine R3.32 regional anchors.

## Ecological-partner governance
- Candidates come only from the 134 R3.21 H0-present species.
- The two sapient-lineage candidates are excluded from partner screening.
- Screening is stratified across all animal guilds still present at 0 ka; apex_carnivore has no surviving H0 species and is not reintroduced. Screening is not a domestication verdict.
- R3.23 latent sociality/life-history/diet/generalism traits provide comparative priors.
- Present H0 component range summaries provide a spatial-availability prior; historical availability is a climate-conditioned backcast, not an observed fossil range.

## Domestication semantics
Domestication is not a Boolean invention. The causal state is continuous:
`contact → management → habituation/tolerance → reproductive control → selective divergence`, with wild gene-flow pressure retained explicitly.
A derived stage code is diagnostic only.

## Critical limitation
WorldSim H0 currently contains animal guilds but no explicit plant species registry. Therefore R3.33 may materialize animal domestication / animal-based food-production support, but:
- plant domestication is forbidden;
- agriculture is forbidden;
- NPP/precipitation represent anonymous plant-resource fields, not plant taxa.

## Non-goals
No named culture, language, religion, city, state, unique human identity, or Deep biological coupling is materialized.
