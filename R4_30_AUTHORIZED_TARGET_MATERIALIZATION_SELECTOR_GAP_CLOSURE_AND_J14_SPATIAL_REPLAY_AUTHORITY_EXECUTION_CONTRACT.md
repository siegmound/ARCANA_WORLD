## R4.30-R1 Authorial Repair Addendum — Authorized Selector Source Binding
The R4.29 authority freezes an exact selector/transform, not a conjunction across all candidate source packets. R4.30-R1 therefore requires one and only one present, parent-hash-matching source packet containing the exact frozen selector. Missing/hash-drifted packets that cannot bind the selector are preserved as provenance warnings and cannot veto that unique exact binding. If no exact valid binding exists, source drift remains fail-closed; if multiple exact bindings exist, materialization remains deferred for ambiguity. No selector, transform, target value, external-engine result or canonical state may be selected or changed based on the observed result.
