# ARCANA WorldSim v0.6D1-R3.39
## Cultural Symbolic Memory, Language Preconditions & Interlineage Identity Dynamics

### Parent authority
R3.39 is downstream of SEALED R3.38. It also consumes SEALED R3.29 community/cultural-precondition authority and the original SEALED R3.20 CHA-2 15–11 ka / 50-year hydrological hazard fields. Parent artifacts are immutable.

### Scientific purpose
Resolve whether distributed managed-forager regional traditions can sustain collective symbolic memory, persistent environmental-event memory, conventional identity marking, and language-divergence preconditions. The stage must distinguish these preconditions from named myths, religions, ethnicities, languages, or language families.

### Window
20 ka -> 0 ka on the exact 145-state R3.32/R3.38 axis, with 9 regional anchors, 12 opaque regional cultural stems, 32 ensemble members, and two sapient lineages. CHA-2 is consumed directly at its exact 80 × 50-year states from 14.95 to 11.00 ka.

### Symbolic-memory semantics
Collective symbolic memory is an abstract capacity/state supported by cultural transmission, retention, social learning, cognition, conventionalized rehearsal, technology/material media, and regional continuity. It is not a claim that a particular myth, story, ritual, ornament, or symbol existed.

CHA-2 event memory is separately seeded from the direct local R3.20 hazard fields sampled at the evolving regional-stem locations. It can decay, be stabilized by rehearsal/encoding, or disappear. No global flood myth is targeted.

### Identity semantics
Identity-marker salience and interlineage-boundary salience are conventional-signalling/affiliation pressures. They are not ethnicities, races, peoples, castes, or named social identities. Contact can promote diffusion or, when coupled to boundary signalling, divergence; isolation is not treated as the only route to differentiation.

### Language semantics
R3.39 materializes communicative conventionalization and language-divergence preconditions only. It does not materialize a named language, language family, grammar, lexicon, or claim that one lineage has a particular terrestrial language structure.

### Governance
- R3.38 cultural genealogy and technology are immutable.
- R3.20 CHA-2 fields are consumed directly and never retuned.
- No lineage-specific rescaling.
- Sensitivity is diagnostic, never a lineage-selection gate.
- No named cultures, languages, religions, ethnicities, states, or Deep/noetic coupling.
