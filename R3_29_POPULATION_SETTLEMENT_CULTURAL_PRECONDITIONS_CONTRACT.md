# ARCANA WorldSim v0.6D1-R3.29 Contract

## Scope
Single-stage population mobility, fission-fusion network, settlement-persistence and cultural-transmission precondition layer over the SEALED R3.28 two-lineage population replay.

## Parent authority
- R3.28 SEALED 35/35.
- Candidate cohort remains `RPT_010_D02`, `RPT_009_D02`.
- R3.23 functional ensemble is read-only input for social/learning/life-history priors.

## Hard governance
- Deep biological coupling OFF.
- No human-similarity target.
- No forced replacement or unique human identity.
- No language, religion, agriculture, city/state, ethnicity, technology inventory or myth is materialized.
- R3.28 `population_proxy` remains a demographic support proxy, not literal census.
- No literal camp headcounts are inferred from the proxy.
- R3.28/H0/CHA-2 and all parent functional states are immutable.

## Time and spatial semantics
The dynamic community layer is computed on the exact R3.28 time states from 50 ka to 0 ka. Spatial community properties are materialized only at R3.28 explicit deme snapshot anchors; no continuous deme path is invented between anchors. CHA-2 disruption is inherited directly from the R3.28 population-weighted 50-y direct hazard consumption.

## Community variables
- residential mobility index
- aggregation potential
- network connectivity
- cultural transmission support
- cumulative cultural-precondition stock
- settlement persistence potential
- resource-stress proxy
- interlineage exchange opportunity
- CHA-2 community disruption

These are dimensionless precondition diagnostics, not observed archaeological quantities.

## Final interpretation
R3.29 retains every lineage that passes the frozen baseline biological/community gates. Sensitivity frequency is a robustness-priority diagnostic, not an extinction or identity gate. A borderline lineage is not discarded merely because it is less stable under stricter thresholds. R3.29 must never assign a final human identity merely because one lineage is more robust.
