# v0.6D1-R3.24 — H1 Functional Human-Readiness Candidate Discovery, Ensemble Robustness & Non-Teleological Shortlist

## Authority boundary

R3.24 consumes the **SEALED R3.23 functional ensemble** read-only. It MUST NOT mutate R3.23, H0, CHA-2, or Deep state.

H1 is the first stage that intentionally asks a human-relevant question. The target is deliberately narrower than "similarity to Homo sapiens":

> Which present lineages have a robust, balanced functional configuration compatible with an open-ended cumulative technological/cultural pathway?

No extant-human measurements, body plan, brain size, bipedality, language score, or author-selected species are used as anchors.

## Discovery axes

Seven primitive functional axes are used:

1. manipulative control;
2. locomotor–effector interface;
3. executive/causal cognition;
4. learning flexibility;
5. social transmission;
6. life-history support for prolonged learning;
7. ecological/dietary flexibility.

`H2_reproductive_output_rate` is not a monotone readiness gate. Tool-use potential and environmental problem solving remain **derived diagnostics**, not direct genetic traits.

## No single readiness scalar

For every R3.23 ensemble member, primitive traits are converted to within-member cross-species percentile ranks. Each axis is the geometric mean of the relevant trait percentiles, which penalizes severe within-axis bottlenecks without setting a Homo threshold.

Candidate ordering is an ordinal consensus across seven non-equivalent diagnostics:

- core balance;
- worst-rate-regime core balance;
- broad threshold-grid robustness;
- Pareto-front frequency;
- worst-rate-regime Pareto frequency;
- derived T/Q median floor;
- worst-rate-regime T/Q floor.

The final ordering uses median diagnostic rank, then mean diagnostic rank, then species ID. The shortlist size (12) is fixed before inspection of the winners.

## Sensitivity

One stage includes all sensitivity checks:

- leave each core axis out once;
- analyze each R3.23 macroevolutionary rate regime independently;
- replace geometric-mean axis aggregation with lower-quartile aggregation;
- remove T/Q derived diagnostics entirely.

Sensitivity does not retroactively tune criteria.

## Interpretation

A shortlisted lineage is **not declared human** and is not absolutely declared human-ready. It is prioritized for H2 detailed biology because it remains competitive under the precommitted multi-dimensional H1 criteria.

## Evidence rationale

The criteria follow comparative cumulative-culture/innovation literature that treats cumulative culture as multi-component, does not require one uniquely human learning mechanism, links prolonged learning to life-history support, and treats innovation as emergent rather than a single scalar trait.
