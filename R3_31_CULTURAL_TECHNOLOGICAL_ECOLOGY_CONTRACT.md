# ARCANA WorldSim — v0.6D1-R3.31 Contract

## Late-Pleistocene → Holocene Cultural-Technological Ecology

Parent authority: **R3.30 SEALED**.

R3.31 converts the sealed census-equivalent, weighted residential-group ABM, community-network history and functional priors into an **abstract functional technological ecology**. It does not assert specific archaeological artifacts, named cultures, languages, religions, agriculture, cities, states or a unique human identity.

### Materialized state

Eight generic technology-domain stocks are replayed for every sealed ensemble member and both retained lineages:

1. portable toolkit systems;
2. composite tool systems;
3. food processing and extraction;
4. thermal/environmental control;
5. shelter and site engineering;
6. transport and logistical systems;
7. storage and resource buffering;
8. cooperative specialization systems.

These are continuous functional repertoire stocks, not claims that a bow, pot, boat, loom, named lithic industry or other specific artifact exists.

The replay also materializes transmission fidelity, innovation opportunity, retention support, repertoire breadth, technical resilience, niche buffering, specialization potential, inter-lineage diffusion opportunity, and innovation/loss flux equivalents.

### Governance

- Parent R3.30 census and group ABM are read-only.
- R3.29 community transmission/network state is read-only.
- R3.23 functional priors are read-only.
- Both lineages remain in the cohort; sensitivity is diagnostic and cannot select a winner.
- No direct CB selection.
- Deep biological coupling remains OFF.
- No language/religion/agriculture/city-state/ethnicity/named-culture materialization.
- No specific artifact is materialized.
- No unique human identity is materialized.

### Interpretation

R3.31 answers: *what kinds of generic technological functions can be accumulated, retained, lost and diffused by the existing communities under their sealed demography, networks and ecology?* It does **not** answer which named technologies or cultures existed.
