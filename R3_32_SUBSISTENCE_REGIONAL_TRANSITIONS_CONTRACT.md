# ARCANA WorldSim — v0.6D1-R3.32 Contract

## Subsistence Intensification, Regional Cultural Lineages & Holocene Transitions

Parent authority: **R3.31 SEALED**.

R3.32 is a derived cultural-ecological stage. It does not modify H0, CHA-2, biology, H3 genetics, R3.28 demography, R3.30 census, or R3.31 technology.

### Materialized

- eight abstract subsistence strategy stocks;
- 20 ka → 0 ka intensification replay;
- broad-spectrum / delayed-return / management / scheduling diagnostics;
- opaque deme-tracked regional cultural-lineage continuity codes;
- managed-resource / food-production transition readiness;
- regional transition pockets as **readiness diagnostics only**;
- 25 sensitivity variants.

### Explicitly not materialized

- agriculture;
- domesticated plant/animal species;
- named cultures, ethnicity, language or religion;
- specific archaeological artifact inventory;
- city/state systems;
- unique human identity;
- Deep biological coupling.

A transition pocket means that storage, management, settlement commitment, continuity and resource intensification jointly support a possible transition. It is **not** a declaration of cultivation, domestication or agriculture.

Regional cultural lineage codes are opaque continuity labels tied to the existing deme history. They are not ethnicities or named cultures.
