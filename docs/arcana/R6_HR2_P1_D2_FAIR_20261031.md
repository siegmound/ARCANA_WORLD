## T0 consistency defect and directional reconstruction

Signed face rate is m3/year from canonical first endpoint to second; the volume
rate is incoming minus outgoing. Original step transfer equals rate*probe_dt
bitwise; applying its stored transfers reconstructs the original updated volume
array bitwise. EW+NS gathers match the total within64eps times incident absolute
rates. Maximum observed grouping residual is3.0517578125e-5m3/year; the original
shared-flux signs and conservation semantics are preserved.

Independently integrated continuum rates are:
EW=0 for latitude; EW=kappa*Dphi*sin(lambda_edge) for longitude;
NS=-kappa*Dlambda*cos(phi_edge)² for latitude;
NS=kappa*cos(phi_edge)*sin(phi_edge)*(sin(lambda_e)-sin(lambda_w)) for longitude.
Their divergence matches -2*kappa/R²*A*(h_exact-H0) within the fixed diagnostic
roundoff budget recorded before execution; maximum absolute residual0.0001220703125
m3/year. These budgets qualify diagnostic algebra, not a loosened V1 criterion.
Full per-cell residual/bound arrays are retained.

The measured consistency defect is original volume rate minus continuum volume
rate, in m3/year. EW/NS directional errors each subtract their exact integrated
face contribution. Their height-rate fields divide by cell area. Cross terms
must be included when combining directional squared L2: their errors are not
additive causal percentages. DIRECTIONAL_CANCELLATION.csv retains EW²,NS²,
2<EW,NS> and the total, including signed identity roundoff.
