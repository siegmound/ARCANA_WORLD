## Thresholds, recentering and inverse conditions

Original design explicitly requires independent integral-enclosure widths
<=2^-50 for M and <=2^-45 for normalized J, plus propagated inverse/flux and
smooth derivative bounds. These are separate obligations. A hull containing
both exact integral and saved approximation would add a requirement; omitting
saved approximation bias would remove one. No width or original Q1 criterion
is changed here. No precision incompatibility has been proved.

Let P=(M^T W M)^-1 M^T W, H=I-1 e_anchor^T. Actual source stores a rounded
product ghat=-Jhat Rhat and evaluates kappa*dot(ghat,h-h_anchor).
Thus the exact represented row is g_eff=ghat H=ghat-e_anchor^T sum(ghat).
The physical sign is F=-kappa J P Hh; first cell loses, second gains the single
shared transfer. This anchoring was in the original design, not a new correction.
Its exact algebra does not eliminate finite subtraction/dot/multiply error.

True inverse certification needs FULL target M/J/W bounds, positive W,
A=M^T W M, B=M^T W, and validated eta=sup||I-C[A]||inf<1. Then inverse and
P-Rhat are bounded by Neumann/residual arguments, followed by J/product/H and
runtime propagation. C2's point witness is not that true-integral certificate;
a mere left inverse is not WLS. RANK_AND_SCALE_ADDENDUM preserves the positive
sigma-lower-bound obligation and the angular-versus-physical rho normalization.
ERROR_BUDGET_AND_WLS_CONDITIONS gives the explicit error decomposition and norms.
