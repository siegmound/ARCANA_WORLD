widths are not an observed integration error; increasing Decimal60 precision

records exact norm/dot defects of its binary64 vectors. Normal/chart/trigonometric

xi derivative is -g(s)/(R*rho), g=s/sin(s). Only normalized J10 and J12 are
nonzero; the other13generic polynomial functionals vanish exactly by geometry
and parity. This is a polynomial-functional certificate, not a fit to the tested
physical harmonic or a modification to the solver.

produce a finite even polynomial P with explicit bound

rational arctan series. Each operation uses exact rational arithmetic followed
by explicit outward integer rounding to a200-bit dyadic lattice. Display floats
are never used as certificate endpoints.
