## Actual timestep and phase semantics

One model year is 31557600 s = 365.25 days; no Gregorian dates or absolute
geological epoch are reconstructed. All 10000 Unified intervals have this
duration and the configured external advance is one year. Regional rain,
runoff, routing, detachment and deposition are annual lumped updates. Manning's
64 bisection iterations are algebraic root iterations, not 64 time substeps.

Full thermal histogram, independently counted from every annual payload:

| Support | Accepted thermal substeps per call | Calls | Nominal accepted step |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| C028 | 1 | 10000 | 31557600 s |
| C029 | 1 | 10000 | 31557600 s |

The kernel can adapt by diffusion stability and kinematic fraction, but it did
not take subannual thermal steps in this run. Stability-trial iterations are
not physical substeps. Min-stability-limit/max-ratio internal diagnostics and
unobserved intermediate states are not archived by the Unified thermal payload.
No hidden subdaily resolution can be claimed from the adaptive method's name.

Hydrology on the two support patches has one global annual substep. The recorded
flow-limiter counter is zero in all 10000 records; this counter would count
limited routing operations, not extra adaptive time integration. The separate
regional basin receives constant 0.15 m/year rain per cell, ET fraction0.10,
infiltration fraction0.15 and runoff fraction0.75. Unified support rainfall,
ET and infiltration forcing are all zero. These are synthetic, different-domain
forcings, not an observed climate. C028 prescribed uplift is 0.0001 m/year,
C029 zero, regional prescribed uplift zero; mechanical opening is 0.01 m/year.
No seasonal forcing, storms or seismic event times were recorded.

**Start and end fields in a Unified record are not simultaneous.** Rheology's
`r6_temperature_k`, `depth_proxy_m`, pressure and E/viscosity are evaluated before
the annual update. `material_volumes.temperature_k/volume_m3`, thermal diagnostics,
strain, surface/hydrology and Deep ledger describe the resulting step. Actual
start/end seconds bound the interval. A0 checked 80 start-of-next-record
temperature/depth joins against T0/Y250..Y9750 checkpoints and 80 end-temperature
joins against Y250..Y10000 checkpoints, all exact.

Thus end-Y temperature profiles are directly stored at Y. End-Y node z values
are present in the start-of-Y+1 rheology payload, with Y10000 supplied by its
native checkpoint. A phase-aware reader can expose these recorded fields without
solving again. It must not label the pre-step depth/T proxy as an end-step value.
This does not certify a bit-exact complete unsaved native ReducedState/checkpoint
at every year: hidden accumulators/geometry arithmetic require a separately
qualified assembler or deterministic replay, which A0 did not execute.

Mechanics uses one quasi-static annual Maxwell/backward-Euler update, not a 3-D
dynamic stress mesh. Its pressure proxy is rho×9.80665×z, not integrated
lithostatic stress or the regional surface-load diagnostic. Deep has only two
reservoir IDs and annual synthetic transport, with no vertical grid or physical
source/heat/work feedback inferred from the 146 thermal/mechanical nodes.

## Multiresolution sampling design and proof

SAMPLING_PLAN.json is the explicit design. The audit constructed only derivative
offset indexes, not a new scientific archive or production query engine:

| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |

Each reference binds original chunk digest, byte offset/span and original
sequence. All year sequences are verified, with nine representative direct byte
seeks at Y0/Y5000/Y10000. Complete regional nondeposit fields for all nine cells,
and the C01/C00-material filtered deposit history, match all nine original saved
snapshot witnesses exactly. No extra full-state series was exported.

Read design:

1. Authenticate the original manifest/contract/B0 view once for a batch and keep
   an explicit verification proof. Select 100Y for overview, 10Y for a window,
   1Y for exact inspection. The source archive remains the same immutable data.
2. Query regional cell fields from the selected year's full-cell-field delta;
   resolve deposit histories by base plus relevant append rows. Proposed
   cell/material posting lists retain material/origin/source/destination/event
   identity and original byte/ordinal provenance. They are not physical remeshing.
3. Query C028/C029 node values by support/node index/material role and explicit
   start/end phase. Keep both arrays where needed; use phase-aware joins rather
   than attaching unlike timestamps. Query Deep by the two actual reservoir IDs.
4. For event-guided windows, select actual kind/cell/material/time-or-NULL, then
   request the union of annual [Y−1,Y+1] windows clipped to the retained horizon.
   Keep `event_time=NULL` and a separate `container_interval`; never replace
   missing time with container Y. Optional threshold/change triggers are new
   analytical annotations, distinct from original physical events.
5. For overview bins, stocks use exact end state; extensive external annual
   flows may be summed in original source order with an explicit aggregator.
   Internal route flows must stay edge-specific or cancel, not be counted as
   new water/material. Temperature min/max are over annual samples, not hidden
   continuous extrema. Every derived aggregate stores units, source range,
   aggregation/version and precision semantics.
6. Reconstruct full regional state on demand, with a separately qualified bounded
   cache keyed by archive/view/hash/schema. A nearest verified checkpoint plus
   suffix replay is a proposed optimization, not a change applied here. Lazy
   native-state loading could avoid the current eager decoded chain, but needs
   explicit integrity-proof retention and bounded parity/memory tests.

Source schema/hash/coverage mismatches must fail closed. Fractional-year or
subcell requests return NO_EXACT_DATA; optional graphical interpolation must be
marked visualization-only, never a new physical state or restart point.

## Memory, output and physical zoom gaps

End-loop resident encoded journal=1620686370 B plus duplicate compact frames
135642542 B, total1756328912 B (1674.966 MiB). End-loop process RSS=2010.816 MiB;
whole-process peak including queries=3795.336 MiB. The encoded objects explain
most growth; the descriptive RSS fit has R²≈0.9997, not an allocation proof or
memory upper bound. Fixed solver objects, container/allocator overhead, checkpoints
and transient copies account for additional memory that was not separately timed.

Journal verification streams one replay buffer; it need not retain all journal
records. The live numerical runtime does retain its encoded history. REC1's
query/recovery loader eagerly retains decoded previous native checkpoints,
which can overlap live entries, replay buffers and selected JSON state objects.
The original cold acceptance process's own VmHWM was2126312 KiB, separately
from the numerical/query process. Shared cgroup peaks include unrelated session
work and are not process RSS or a reservation. No free-memory gate was added.

Original run output:2469842406 logical B/2470232064 allocated B,219 files;
journal1621296371 B, native checkpoints739013891 B, B0 metadata534919 B.
Nine full snapshot files total about98.55 MB; invocation output also includes
annual telemetry. No10001-state legacy export was generated. The A0 indexes
add only derivative references in the new audit directory.

Existing data support exact annual public-payload views, arbitrary annual
regional snapshots, stored node temperatures/phase-aware depths, annual Deep
and material histories, and the 40 actual native restart states. They cannot
recover new subannual event times, storms, intrancell channels, a finer vertical
mesh, 3-D stress, mineral composition or absent climate/ecology/population fields.
Interpolation or a denser view is not higher physical resolution.

Before a future scientific zoom, separate gates must address:

- Authored georeferenced nesting and conservative area/volume mappings between
  the synthetic basin, 1° support footprints and 1 m mechanical slice; qualified
  terrain and hydraulic/contact boundary laws, including C04/C05.
- Finer vertical material profiles, an explicit physical/model-base definition,
  calibrated rheology/mineralogy and spatial/time convergence. Smaller thermal
  spacing changes explicit diffusion stability approximately with spacing².
- Finer forcing and independently resolved event timing; appropriate hydrological
  and mechanical steps, new stored intermediate states and uncertainty/error
  measures. Replaying old annual forcing does not invent hourly forcing.
- Physically justified multiway coupling. Regional geomorphic load is presently
  a bookkeeping diagnostic; it is not fed into column thermal/mechanics/Deep.
  Unified Deep has synthetic two-reservoir exchange with zero observed geological/
  solar/loss source arrays and declared zero thermal/work transfers. It does not
  establish regional boundary Deep transport or a planetary energy closure.
- New qualified providers/models for missing weathering/lithification, chemistry,
  climate, ecology and populations if those are actually required. Their current
  absence is explicit, not a retrospective zero-valued observation.

Any higher physical resolution needs a separately authorized and qualified new
model/run. A next read-only query/index/cache implementation could reuse this
archive, but must separately qualify hash/phase parity, bounded memory, NULL
semantics and batched costs. A0 qualifies this audit and design, not a new solver,
production query engine, sampling policy ratification or scientific zoom.
