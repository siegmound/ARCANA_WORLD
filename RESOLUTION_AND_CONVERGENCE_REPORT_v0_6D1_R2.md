## Decision

250 kyr is accepted as a **macroevolution pilot cadence**, because global and abundance-weighted species-level quantities are sufficiently stable for this 30 Myr validation interval. Fine cell-level occupancy remains substantially more resolution-sensitive and is explicitly **NOT SEALED**. Production deep-time replay should decouple an internal transport cadence from external chunk/checkpoint cadence before a raster-resolution seal.
