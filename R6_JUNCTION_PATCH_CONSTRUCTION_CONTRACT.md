# Junction patch construction contract

A future patch must be one connected local region in the same global spherical arrangement as its three incident corridors, preserve exactly the parent junction/boundary/plate identities, and have no holes, self-intersections or fourth incident branch. Its extent must derive from the incident-zone/network geometry; an independent arbitrary radius is not authorized.

Incidence alone does not authorize a motion rule. This contract covers only static reference geometry. All 20 patches remain unresolved until the spherical arrangement and its junction fixture pass.
