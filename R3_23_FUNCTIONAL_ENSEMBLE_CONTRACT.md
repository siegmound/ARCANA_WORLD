## Why an ensemble is required

H0 tracked ecology, population, genealogy and the governed three-trait reduced genetic state, but did **not** historically materialize anatomy, cognition, learning or social behaviour. Therefore the 31 R3.22 functional coordinates are latent/underdetermined conditional on H0.

R3.23 must not invent a unique retrospective history and call it observed canon. Instead it materializes a reproducible **comparative-prior ensemble** conditioned on:

- the fixed H0 lineage tree;
- lineage birth/extinction timing;
- legacy functional guild history;
- present component body/ecological trait proxies;
- generation-time proxies;
- current range breadth, component structure and H0 event history;
- comparative multi-taxon evidence classes.

The ensemble represents epistemic + macroevolutionary uncertainty. H1 must later prefer candidates that are robust across this uncertainty rather than winners of one random realization.

## Important semantic limit

This is a **macroevolutionary latent functional replay**, not a retrospective claim that H0 had already simulated unrecorded neural/morphological microstate.

R3.23 therefore does not materialize or pretend to know:

- component-level functional G-matrices;
- exact within-population additive variance for all 31 traits;
- plasticity matrices;
- detailed anatomical innovations not represented in H0;
- human similarity or sapience.

Those remain downstream zoom/replay quantities if a lineage becomes H1-relevant.

### Applicability / anatomy safeguard

H0 does not contain a historical anatomical-innovation ledger rich enough to infer exact `ACTIVE / STRUCTURAL_ZERO / NOT_APPLICABLE` transitions for all 31 coordinates. R3.23 therefore does **not** fabricate `functional_applicability_code`. Its latent z-values are conditional functional potentials within the generic D1 macrofaunal scope. Low values can represent poor functional realization, but R3.23 does not claim that an unrecorded anatomical innovation occurred. The two derived capabilities are conditional potentials, not observed tool behaviour.

## Comparative evidence classes

The calibration registry uses broad, non-human comparative authorities including:

- AVONET — global avian morphology/ecology;
- EltonTraits — bird/mammal diet, foraging stratum, activity and body size;
- PanTHERIA — mammal ecology/life-history traits;
- Amniote Life-History Database — birds, mammals and reptiles;
- Mousseau & Roff / later meta-analyses — cross-trait heritability patterns;
- Dochtermann et al. 2019 — phylogenetically controlled behavioural heritability meta-analysis;
- Atlas of Comparative Cognition — systematic comparative cognition evidence coverage;
- Animal Culture Database — socially transmitted behaviour evidence.

Humans do not define any scale or target.

## Heritability priors

Heritability values are used only as **evidence metadata / uncertainty class calibration**, not as direct component `functional_va`.

Reference central tendencies:

- morphology-like functional traits: `h² ≈ 0.461`;
- behavioural/cognitive/social traits: `h² ≈ 0.235`;
- foraging behaviour: `h² ≈ 0.196`;
- life-history traits: `h² ≈ 0.262`.

This avoids falsely equating cross-study heritability with standardized within-component additive variance.

## Root priors

The six historical WorldSim guilds are used only as weak 210 Ma ecological strata:

1. small generalist herbivore;
2. large browser;
3. medium low-vegetation feeder;
4. small reptiloid generalist;
5. medium carnivore;
6. apex carnivore.

Guild affects weak prior centres through generic descriptors (body-size class, generalism, active predation, trophic mode). Root prior uncertainty is deliberately larger than these shifts.

No named lineage receives a special prior.

## Phylogenetic replay

Each ensemble member replays the 348-species historical tree in chronological birth order.

Daughters inherit the parent state at the authorized birth time. Trait change between births follows a bounded Ornstein-Uhlenbeck/Brownian macroevolutionary prior with three rate regimes:

- `CONSERVATIVE`;
- `BASELINE`;
- `LABILE`.

Mutation/innovation noise has zero mean. No lineage receives a directional push toward tool use, cognition, sapience or human similarity.

## Present component conditioning

Proxy loadings are deliberately small relative to ensemble uncertainty. Cognitive/social dimensions are not deterministically inferred from body size or guild.
