# Cross-engine population-scale contract — R3.6C

WorldSim population values are effective macrofaunal abundance units; NEMO simulates literal individuals. A one-to-one substitution would change drift strength by many orders of magnitude.

R3.6C therefore forbids interpreting the NEMO benchmark population count as the literal effective population represented by an ARCANA WorldSim population unit.

Primary cross-engine evidence is control-normalized (`flow minus no-flow`) and must include population-size sensitivity before any biological calibration is proposed. Absolute `VA` level agreement alone is not a promotion criterion.
