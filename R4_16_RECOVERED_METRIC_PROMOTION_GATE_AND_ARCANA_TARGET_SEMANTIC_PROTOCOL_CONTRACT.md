## Gate B — ARCANA target semantics
All 59 P1 cells receive one of the 15 frozen domain semantic protocols. A filename/source hit is only a source candidate. A valid ARCANA target must later expose, explicitly:
1. value or statistic definition;
2. unit/normalization;
3. temporal basis;
4. spatial support;
5. population/lineage/guild semantics;
6. deterministic transform from canonical source artifact;
7. provenance and uncertainty/aggregation convention where applicable.

External-engine results may never define or tune the ARCANA target.
