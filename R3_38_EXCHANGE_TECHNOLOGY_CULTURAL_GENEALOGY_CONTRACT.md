# ARCANA WorldSim v0.6D1-R3.38
## Regional Forager Exchange Networks, Concrete Technology & Cultural Genealogies

### Parent authority
R3.38 is downstream of SEALED R3.37 and may execute only while both sapient lineages resolve to `DISTRIBUTED_MANAGED_FORAGER_ECONOMY`. It also consumes SEALED R3.31 technological ecology, R3.32 opaque regional cultural lineages, and R3.33 environmental/animal-resource affordances. Parent artifacts are immutable.

### Scientific purpose
Translate abstract technological capacity into concrete functional implementation families that are physically and ecologically supportable, then resolve regional exchange and reticulate cultural genealogy (vertical continuity plus horizontal borrowing) without inventing named cultures, languages, religions, cities, markets or states.

### Window
20 ka -> 0 ka on the exact 145-state R3.32/R3.37 axis, with 9 regional anchors and the exact 32-member / 2-lineage ensemble.

### Concrete implementation families
R3.38 tracks 12 functional implementation classes: hard-edge mineral cutting/processing; hafted composite points/edges; cordage/binding/netting; hide/skin flexible thermal goods; osseous hard-biological implements; grinding/pounding food processing; storage containers/cache systems; built shelter/site furniture; aquatic capture gear; overland logistical carriers; waterborne transport systems; adhesive/binding compounds.

These are functional implementation families, not direct archaeological observations, named artifact traditions, or claims that a particular terrestrial object existed.

### Material authority and limitation
Biotic, hydroclimatic and coastal material affordances are inherited spatially from R3.33 and group locations from R3.32/R3.37. High-resolution lithic/raw-mineral quality maps are not yet an authority, so hard-mineral availability is represented by an explicit global prior and cannot support claims about named lithic sources, ores or metallurgy.

### Exchange network
Each ensemble member contains 12 regional cultural stems: six opaque R3.32 regional codes for each sapient lineage. Exchange edges depend on inherited group positions, R3.37 exchange access, spatial separation and interlineage exchange support. Edge values are dimensionless material/information-flow support, not literal trade tonnage.

### Cultural genealogy
The genealogy is reticulate rather than forced into a pure tree. Same-stem continuity provides vertical ancestry; exchange can create horizontal borrowing/blending edges. Fission and fusion are materialized only if ensemble-robust pressure gates are reached. Opaque regional codes remain non-ethnic, unnamed analytical identities.

### Prohibited materialization
R3.38 does not materialize named cultures, ethnicity, language families, religions, metallurgy, currency, markets, villages, cities, states, unique-human identity, or Deep/noetic biological coupling.

### Governance
- R3.37 pathway is immutable.
- No lineage-specific rescaling.
- No direct archaeological-observation claims.
- No metallurgy without a raw-material/process authority.
- Sensitivity is diagnostic, never a lineage-selection gate.
