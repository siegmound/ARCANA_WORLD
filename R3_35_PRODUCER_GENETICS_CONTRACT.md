# v0.6D1-R3.35 — Producer Heritable Variation, Selection Response & Domestication Genetics

## Purpose
Replace the phenomenological `selective_divergence` proxy of R3.34 with an explicit quantitative-genetic producer state. R3.35 keeps all 36 R3.34 Producer Operational Taxa and replays 20 ka→0 ka with additive genetic variance, multivariate response to selection, wild↔managed gene-flow pressure, variance depletion/replenishment, and coevolutionary feedback to controlled propagation.

## Parent authority

- The 36 producer taxa, R3.34 resource landscape, R3.34 human/producer contact-management history and R3.34 domestication gate thresholds are immutable parent authority.
- No retroactive flora phylogeny is asserted.

## Genetic state
Six standardized domestication-response axes are materialized per member × sapient lineage × producer taxon:
1. propagule-size shift;
2. dormancy reduction;
3. reduced shattering/dispersal;
4. maturation/phenology shift;
5. edible-yield allocation shift;
6. defense/toxicity reduction.

For each axis R3.35 stores a managed-population mean shift, additive variance and effective narrow-sense heritability. The multivariate response uses a positive-semidefinite additive covariance matrix and a weak per-generation selection gradient:

`Δz = G β × generations`

Wild gene-flow pressure attenuates response and pulls managed means toward the wild reference. Mutation/recombination supply and wild introgression replenish variance; directional selection can deplete it. No CB/H3 state participates.

## Coevolutionary feedback
R3.34 propagation control is the immutable baseline, not the final human behavior. If genetically altered producer populations yield a stronger domestication phenotype, managed groups may increase effective propagation control and dependency. This feedback is causal and symmetric across the two sapient lineages; it cannot reduce the R3.34 baseline.

## Domestication gates
R3.35 inherits the R3.34 incipient and functional-domestication thresholds exactly. The only substitution is that the R3.34 phenomenological divergence variable is replaced by the explicit genetic-divergence index computed from the six quantitative traits. Thresholds are never relaxed after observing results.
