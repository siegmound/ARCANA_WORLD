# R6 plate kinematics parameter evidence matrix

This is evidence for priors and interface semantics, not a parameter set for a generated trajectory. Earth observations are not copied into R6. `RMS` is retained as used by each cited analysis; do not conflate it with instantaneous boundary-normal opening rate.

## Source register

- **Zahirovic et al. 2015**, “Tectonic speed limits from plate kinematic reconstructions,” *Earth and Planetary Science Letters* 418, 40–52, DOI `10.1016/j.epsl.2015.02.037`. Primary Mesozoic–Cenozoic reconstruction analysis. Transfer: Earth-scale plausibility only; not individual synthetic-plate calibration.
- **Argus 1991**, “No-net-rotation model of current plate velocities incorporating plate motion model NUVEL-1,” *Geophysical Research Letters*, DOI `10.1029/91GL01532`. Primary frame model. Transfer: declared gauge convention only; source itself notes the NNR physical premise can be dynamically incorrect.
- **DeMets et al. 1994**, “Effect of recent revisions to the geomagnetic reversal time scale on estimates of current plate motions,” *Geophysical Research Letters*, DOI `10.1029/94GL02118`. Primary recalibration of present-day Earth relative-motion model. Transfer: illustrates explicit calibration, not R6 initial motion.
- **Iaffaldano, Bodin & Sambridge 2012**, “Reconstructing plate-motion changes in the presence of finite-rotations noise,” *Nature Communications* 3, 1048, DOI `10.1038/ncomms2051`. Primary statistical analysis. Transfer: temporal-resolution/noise caution only.
- **Brune et al. 2023**, “Geodynamics of continental rift initiation and evolution,” *Nature Reviews Earth & Environment* 4, 235–253, DOI `10.1038/s43017-023-00391-3`. Review. Transfer: mechanisms and uncertainty, not a universal threshold.
- **Corti 2009**, “Continental rift evolution: From rift initiation to incipient break-up in the Main Ethiopian Rift, East Africa,” *Earth-Science Reviews* 96, 1–53, DOI `10.1016/j.earscirev.2009.06.005`. Regional synthesis. Transfer: example of inherited weakness plus far-field extension; not a global prior.
- **pyGPlates 1.0.0 official FiniteRotation API** and **Müller et al. 2018**, “GPlates: Building a Virtual Earth Through Deep Time,” *Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems*, DOI `10.1029/2018GC007584`. Software semantics and methods, not motion-law authority.

Publisher/DOI destinations and official API documentation were checked during the task on 2026-09-24. No blog, forum, tutorial default or software default was used as physical authority. No source values were averaged across incompatible populations or scales.
