# R6 boundary-zone width evidence matrix

| Candidate | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| A_SINGLE_GLOBAL_BOUNDED_MODEL_SCALE | PREFERRED_MINIMUM_PRIOR_CANDIDATE | Avoids unsupported type-specific distinctions; a bounded authorial envelope is explicit, but not yet a physical canonical field. |
| B_CRUST_PAIR_CONDITIONED | NOT_AVAILABLE | The current boundary records do not govern a crust-pair classification for each segment. |
| C_KINEMATIC_MODE_CONDITIONED | NOT_SELECTED | Opening/closing/shear are diagnostics, not geological boundary-type evidence; no source validates mapping mode to width. |
| D_SEEDED_SPATIALLY_CORRELATED_FIELD | UNNECESSARY_COMPLEXITY_AT_THIS_STAGE | No support exists for correlation length or covariance; adding them would invent parameters. |
| E_ADAPTIVE_NUMERICAL_WIDTH | NUMERICAL_ONLY_NOT_PHYSICAL | Cannot stand in for W_model or produce physical strain authority. |

The proposed authorial envelope is 100,000–1,000,000 m, with no distribution. Its endpoints are not empirical limits. Chen & Grimison report several-hundred-kilometre extents in oceanic diffuse deformation examples; Gordon reports some diffuse zones exceeding 1,000 km on a side (not necessarily cross-boundary width). These contextual observations do not calibrate a universal ARCANA parameter.
