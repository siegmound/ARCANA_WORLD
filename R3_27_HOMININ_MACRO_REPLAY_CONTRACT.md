# ARCANA WorldSim v0.6D1-R3.27 — Hominin Macro-Evolution Replay to 200 ka

R3.27 is one substantive single stage. It consumes the six H3-compatible lineages sealed in R3.26 and creates a **derived recent sublineage fork** for each candidate from 3.0 Ma to 200 ka. It never rewrites the H0 natural-control history.

## Core rule

The six candidates receive the **same environmental forcing per ensemble member**. No lineage-specific CB rescaling, no Homo-similarity objective, no human body-plan target, and no Deep biological coupling are permitted.

## Ensemble

- 96 members: 32 mild, 32 baseline, 32 high-variability forcing histories.
- 3.0 Ma → 0.2 Ma.
- 20 kyr macrosteps.
- Environmental variability and habitat corridors vary within precommitted broad priors. Bottleneck forcing is an explicit mild/moderate/severe mixture because the proposed ~0.9 Ma severe bottleneck is empirically contested; no severe event is forced in every replay.
- H2 modules and H0 demographic/range support determine lineage response; H3 remains the universal neutral reference bridge.

## HUMAN_200KA semantics

`HUMAN_200KA` is a **macroevolutionary candidate checkpoint**, not a final taxonomic identity. A lineage must satisfy all frozen demographic/robustness gates and remain qualified in at least 70% of sensitivity variants. Multiple lineages may qualify; no unique winner is forced.

## Evidence use

Comparative hominin evidence is used to justify model architecture and broad priors only: Pleistocene climate variability/habitat tracking, long-lived population structure and gene flow, plausible low effective population sizes, and severe bottleneck scenarios. Earth Homo values are not used as a lineage-specific target.
